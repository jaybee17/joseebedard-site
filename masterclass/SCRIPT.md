# Free Masterclass Script — "How I Replaced Myself With AI Employees"

60 minutes. Recorded once, watched forever — this is a YouTube video, not a live webinar. Simple words, short sentences, kitchen-table talk. No hype, no fake urgency, no income promises. Record with Voicebox narration over the animated deck (youtube-video-factory pipeline), or as a straight-to-camera talk — either works with this script.

## Open (0:00–5:00) — The Hook

- Who I am, one line: 58, no coding background, ran a content business alone until it broke me.
- The promise of the hour: "In the next 60 minutes I'm going to show you the exact system I built — the same one my content business runs on today."
- What this is NOT: not a course pitch, not a get-rich scheme. It's the real system, free.
- Tease the end: "Stick around, because at the end I'll show you where I hired my first AI employee — and where you can build yours, today."

## Part 1 (5:00–15:00) — Why I Had to Change

- The agency years: seven years, big clients, on call 24/7, no boundaries.
- The collapse: ICU, the question that had no answer — "what's the point of building a business if it breaks you?"
- The decision to rebuild differently: calm, intentional, on my own terms.
- Turning point: sitting down with Claude for the first time.

## Part 2 (15:00–25:00) — The Brain File

- What a brain file is: one document that teaches AI your story, your people, and how you sound.
- Why skipping this step gets you robot content, no matter how good your prompts are.
- Walk through the real questions I answered to build mine (story, audience, voice, tone, off-limits topics).
- The result: everything my AI employees write sounds like me, not a template.

## Part 3 (25:00–42:00) — Meet the Employees

- The idea: AI employees, not AI tools. Each one has ONE job, does it the same way every time, in your voice.
- Walk through mine, one at a time, plain language:
  1. **Post Writer** — turns one idea into one finished post.
  2. **Image Maker** — designs the graphics that go with every post.
  3. **Reel Scripter** — turns an idea into a short-form video script.
  4. **Viral Spotter** — watches for what's working and flags outliers worth learning from.
  5. **Brand Reviewer** — the quality gate; checks every piece before it goes out.
  6. **Scheduler** — queues everything to post at the right time.
  7. **Video Editor** — assembles and polishes finished video content.
- For each: what it does, what it does NOT do, and where the line is (I still decide and approve — the AI never publishes on its own).

## Part 4 (42:00–50:00) — The 90/10 Routine

- The daily shape of my workday: Decide. Approve. Press publish.
- Walk through one real morning, start to finish, with the employees doing their part.
- What never gets automated: answering my own community, my own voice on camera, my own decisions. AI writes my content — it never answers for me.

## Part 5 (50:00–57:00) — Where You Start

- You don't build seven employees on day one. You build one.
- The first employee to hire if you've never opened Claude: the Post Writer (simplest job description, fastest payoff).
- A plain-language starting prompt, read out loud so it's easy to copy.
- Reassurance for the "I'm not techy" fear: this is typing sentences to a chat window, not code.

## Close (57:00–60:00) — What's Next

- Recap the whole system in one breath: brain file → employees → 90/10 routine.
- Where to go next: the free community, where I answer questions myself and share the free guides (Brain File guide, AI Employee System guide, Carousel System guide).
- One clear next step, said once, no pressure: "Join free, grab the guides, and build your first employee this week."
