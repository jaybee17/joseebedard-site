Questions

Good questions

Do I need any tech skills?

No. If you can type your name and pick a color, you can use the Library. The books and sales pages come out finished.

Can I really put my own name on these books?

Yes. That is exactly what the Library is for. The license gives you the right to brand each book as your own and sell it.

Will other people be selling the same books?

Other members can brand the same titles, but every copy carries its owner's name, brand, and colors, and you can retitle yours. Your audience buys from you because they know you.

How do I actually sell them?

The Library does the heavy lifting. Each book comes with a finished sales page, plus a ready-to-paste shop listing for wherever you sell (Payhip, Etsy, Gumroad, Beacons, Stan Store): title, description, and tags, with copy buttons. And the "Where to sell" guide inside walks you through setting up your first shop, step by step, even if you've never sold anything online.

Will I make money with this?

That depends on you. The Library gives you ready products and pages, which is the fastest possible start. Selling them still takes showing up for your audience. No one can promise you income, and you should not trust anyone who does.

What if new books come out after I buy?

They're yours. The library keeps growing, and members get every new book at no extra cost.
