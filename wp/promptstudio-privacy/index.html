What we collect

When you buy, payment is handled by WarriorPlus and its payment providers. We receive your name, email address and purchase details so we can deliver your product and provide support. We do not receive your full card details.

Prompt Studio

The voice profile and settings you enter in Prompt Studio are stored only in your own web browser on your own device. They are not sent to our servers. Photos you add on the Design tab stay on your device and are not uploaded.

How we use your information

To deliver your purchase, give support, process refunds, and, if you agree, send updates about our products. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing

We share information only with the services needed to run the business (such as WarriorPlus, our email provider Brevo, and GitHub Pages, which hosts this site), or when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

These pages set no cookies and run no analytics of their own. They load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them. WarriorPlus and GitHub Pages may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing us.

Changes
