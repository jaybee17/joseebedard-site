Why I Made Claude Made Simple

From the desk of Josée Bédard

I'm not a tech person. I started out as a rural mail carrier. Like a lot of people, I tried every "get rich quick" thing online and earned nothing.

Then I stopped chasing and started building. I made my own digital products and promoted offers I believed in. Over the years I've sold more than 30,000 digital products, and I built a full marketing agency on top of it.

That agency nearly broke me. Eighty-hour weeks. On call all the time. In the end my body gave out and I landed in the ICU. I shut it all down.

When AI got good, I rebuilt, calmly and on my own terms. I started using Claude, and at first I did what everyone does: I typed one vague line, got a bland answer, and closed the tab. It didn't save me any time at all.

What changed was giving Claude the right information up front: who I am, who I help, how I sound, and exactly what I want. I taught it my story and my voice, then built helpers that write, design and schedule most of my content. Work that used to take me days now takes hours. Today AI does about 90% of the work in my business, and I read it, fix it and approve it. That is how I got my time back.

Here is the part nobody tells you: the time you save comes from the prompt, not the tool. A good prompt gets you a good first draft. A weak one leaves you rewriting everything yourself. Prompt Studio builds the good prompt for you, so you skip the guessing.

No jargon. No coding. Just clear steps and ready-made prompts a complete beginner can follow the same day. My story is my own and is not a promise of your results, but I know the blank screen is where most people stop. This gets you past it.
