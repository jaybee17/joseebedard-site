1What Claude is (and isn't)

2 minutes to read

Claude is an AI assistant you chat with, like texting a very fast helper. You type what you want, and it writes back. It can draft posts, emails and scripts, explain things simply, brainstorm ideas and tidy up your writing.

Think of it like this

Claude is a helpful assistant who has never met you. The better you describe what you want, the better the result. That's all a "prompt" is: your instructions to the assistant.

What Claude is not

  • Not perfect. It can make mistakes, so always read and edit what it writes.
  • Not a mind reader. It only knows what you tell it. That's why Prompt Studio asks you a few questions first.
  • Not a money button. It helps you create faster. What you do with it, and your results, are up to you.

2Set up Claude

About 5 minutes
  1. Go to claude.ai in your web browser.
  2. Create an account or sign in.
  3. Click New chat. You'll see a message box at the bottom. That's where you type or paste.

3Your first win in 5 minutes

5 minutes

Before you open Prompt Studio, try something tiny so you can see how it works. Paste this into Claude and press send:

Write a friendly 3-line post I can share on Facebook about a small win I had this week. Keep it simple and warm. Give me 3 different versions.

Claude replies in seconds. Now try a follow-up. Type:

Make the second version shorter and add one question at the end.

That's the whole idea: ask, read, then refine. You're already using Claude.

4Meet Prompt Studio

3 minutes

Writing good prompts takes practice. Prompt Studio does that part for you. You answer a few easy questions and it builds a complete prompt to paste into Claude.

5Your first three tasks

15 minutes

Do these in order. Each one takes about five minutes.

Task 1: A social post

  1. Pick Social media post.
  2. Write what the post is about, choose where you'll post it, and pick a goal.
  3. Copy, paste into Claude, choose your favourite of the three versions, and edit it so it sounds like you.

Task 2: A short email

  1. Pick Email and choose "Welcome email".
  2. Say what it's about and what the reader should do next.
  3. Copy, paste, and choose the best subject line from the five Claude gives you.

Task 3: A week of ideas

  1. Pick 7-day content plan.
  2. Enter your topic and where you post.
  3. Copy, paste, and you'll have a full week of ideas in a table.
You did it. You now have a post, an email and a week of ideas. That's a great first session.

6How to get better answers

3 minutes to read
  • Give Claude details. "A post about dog training" is weaker than "a post for new puppy owners about the first week at home".
  • Say who it's for. Claude writes differently for beginners than for experts.
  • Ask for options. "Give me 3 versions" is the easiest way to find one you like.
  • Refine, don't restart. Keep chatting in the same conversation and say what to change.
  • Edit the result. Add your own stories and details. That's what makes it yours.

Follow-ups that always help

Make it shorter.Make it sound more like me and less like AI.
Give me 3 different versions.Make the opening line stronger.
Simplify the language for a complete beginner.Add a clear call to action at the end.

Prompt Studio has these as one-click copy buttons.

7Use it safely and honestly

2 minutes to read
Always check before you publish. Claude can sound confident and still be wrong. Read everything, and fix anything that isn't true.
  • Don't paste private information. No passwords, bank details or other people's personal data.
  • Don't invent proof. Never publish made-up results, reviews or income claims. If you didn't earn it, don't say it.
  • Be clear about what you offer. What you write should match what people really get.
  • Make it yours. Add your real stories and experience.
