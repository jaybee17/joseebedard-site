SCROLL INTO MY STORY
The Journey

No shortcuts. No luck. Here is how it actually went.

From a rural mail route to a business run by AI employees.

THE EARLY DAYS

The mail route

I was a rural mail carrier. Like a lot of people, I tried every "get rich quick" thing out there — and earned nothing. I was broke, discouraged, and convinced online business wasn't for me.

THE FIRST WIN

I stopped chasing. I started building.

I quit the hype and learned real skills: my own digital products, plus offers I actually believed in. That combination finally worked.

Over the years: 30,000+ digital products sold and a spot in the top 3% of affiliates on the platforms I worked.

THE AGENCY YEARS

Seven years of success that almost broke me

I built a full digital marketing agency. Big clients. Industry recognition. And the price: on call 24/7, 80-hour weeks, no boundaries. My business became a burden.

"What's the point of building a business if it breaks you?"
THE COLLAPSE

My body gave out

I burned out completely. I collapsed, and I landed in the ICU. In that hospital bed I asked myself the question above — and I couldn't answer it.

So I shut it all down.

THE REBUILD

Rebuilding, quietly, with AI

I rebuilt differently this time: calmly, intentionally, on my terms. When AI got good, everything changed. I sat down with Claude, taught it my story and my voice, and built my brain file.

Then I created my AI employees: a post writer, an image maker, a scheduler, a scripter, and more. Seven employees. Zero payroll.

NOW

Sharing what I know, freely

Today I create content full time. My AI employees do 90% of the work, I approve the 10%, and I press publish. Decide. Approve. Press publish.

I share everything I learn inside my free community — and I answer every question there myself. AI writes my content. It never answers for me.

Why listen to me

The work behind the advice is real.

I'm not a guru selling dreams. I'm a creator who has built, lost, rebuilt, and learned from every step — including getting wiped out and starting from zero more than once.
