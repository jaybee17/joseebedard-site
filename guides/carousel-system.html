My Carousel System

I post a carousel every day and never open a design app. Here's the system, start to finish.

A carousel is just slides with words on them. The reason most look messy is that every post starts from zero: new colors, new fonts, new layout. Mine never do. I made the design decisions once, wrote them down, and now my AI employees make every carousel inside those rules.

Step 1 — Lock your three colors, forever

Pick three: a background, a text color, one accent. Mine are cream, black, and gold — every post I make uses those three and nothing else. That's why my page looks like one brand instead of six. Write them down with their exact codes so your AI can use them too.

Step 2 — Lock your two fonts

One bold font for the big words. One quiet font for the small words. That's it — two jobs, two fonts. Add them to the same note as your colors. You now have a brand kit, and it took five minutes.

Step 3 — Give the kit to your AI employee

Open your Claude project (the one with your brain file) and paste your kit in:

These are my brand rules for every carousel: Colors: [background], [text], [accent] — never any others. Fonts: [bold font] for headlines, [quiet font] for body. Slide 1 is always a big hook. The last slide always tells people the one next step. Save this as a permanent rule.

Step 4 — Ask for the whole carousel in one go

Now the daily part takes minutes. One prompt:

Write me a 7-slide carousel about [topic] in my voice. Slide 1: a hook that stops the scroll — no clickbait, just true. Slides 2-6: one idea per slide, short lines, plain words. Slide 7: the next step. Then write the caption to go with it.

You get the words for every slide, in your voice, inside your brand rules. Put them on your template and it's done. (Inside my community I show how my AI employee builds the finished images too.)

Step 5 — Run the quality gate

Before anything gets posted, I check three things: Does slide 1 make ME want to swipe? Does every slide sound like me out loud? Are there only my three colors and two fonts on it? Three yeses, and I press publish. Any no, and it goes back for one fix.

That's the system. Decide once, write it down, let your AI employee work inside the rules, and approve the 10% at the end. A daily carousel without a design app, without a designer, and without it ever looking like anyone else's page.
