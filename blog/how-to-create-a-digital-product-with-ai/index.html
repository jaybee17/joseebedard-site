How to Create a Digital Product With AI, Step by Step

I have sold over 30,000 digital products. Here is the simple process I would follow today, with an AI helper doing the heavy lifting.

By Josée Bédard · November 26, 2026

What a digital product is

A digital product is something people get as a file or online, with nothing to ship. A guide, a checklist, a set of templates, a planner. You make it once, and many people can use it.

I have sold over 30,000 of them over the years. I will not promise you that kind of result, because every person and every product is different. But I can show you the simple steps I follow.

Step 1: Pick one person and one problem

Do not make something for "everyone". Write one sentence:

This helps [one kind of person] with [one problem].

For example: "This helps women over 50 plan a calm first week of a small online business." That is clear. "Business tips for everyone" is not.

Step 2: Pick a simple format

Start with the easiest one:

  • A short guide (10 to 20 pages) that walks someone through steps
  • A checklist or planner they can print
  • A set of templates they can fill in

A short guide is the easiest place to start. You can make the others later.

Step 3: Teach your AI helper who you are

If you skip this, your product will sound like every other AI product. Do the short interview that creates your brain file first. I explain exactly how in What Is a Brain File?. It takes about 20 minutes and it is the biggest quality upgrade you can make.

Step 4: Make an outline

Ask Claude for a simple outline of five or six parts. Paste this and fill in the brackets:

Using what you know about me, write an outline for a short guide that helps [who] with [problem]. Give me five or six parts, each with a one-line description. Keep the words simple.

Read the outline. Cut anything that is not needed. Move things around. A short guide people finish is better than a long one they do not.

Step 5: Write one part at a time

Do not ask for the whole guide at once. Go one part at a time:

Now write part 1: [name of the part]. Use short sentences and plain words. Include one real example. Keep it to about 300 words. Do not promise any results.

Read each part out loud. Anything that does not sound like you, change it.

Step 6: Add your real experience

This is the part only you can do, and it is what makes your product worth buying. In every part, add:

  • One true story, or what happened to you
  • One mistake you made, so the reader can skip it
  • One small thing you wish someone had told you

AI writes the first draft. You make it real.

Step 7: Check it carefully

AI makes mistakes, so this step is not optional:

  • Check every fact, date, name and number.
  • Remove any claim you cannot back up, especially about money, health or results.
  • Make sure it does not copy someone else's work. Everything should be in your own words.
  • Read the whole thing once, start to finish.

Step 8: Put it into one simple file

Put it in a clean, simple document and save it as a PDF. Add a title, a short welcome, the parts and a thank-you at the end. Keep it simple and easy to read. Big, clear text matters more than fancy design.

Step 9: Test it with a few real people

Before you share it widely, give it to three to five people who match your "who". Ask:

  • What helped you most?
  • What was confusing?
  • What is missing?

Fix what they tell you. This step is the difference between a product that works and one that does not.

Step 10: Share it honestly

Decide where people will get it, like a simple page or an online shop. Write a short, honest description of what it is, who it is for and what it does not do. Do not promise income or results. Honest descriptions build trust.

Common mistakes

  1. Making it too big. Start small and finish it.
  2. Skipping your voice. If it sounds like AI, people feel it.
  3. Not checking facts. One wrong fact can lose a reader's trust.
  4. Waiting to be perfect. Finish a good first version, then improve it.
  5. Promising results. Be honest about what the product can and cannot do.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

A short guide can take a few days of small sessions, with AI doing the first drafts. Do not rush the checking.

Do I need a website?

Not to make the product. You can start by sharing it with a few people and learn from them.

Can I sell something made with AI?

Many people do. What matters is that it is accurate, helpful, in your own voice and not copied from anyone.

What should I charge?

Start with a fair, small price you are comfortable with, and learn from how people respond. I will not give you a number, because it depends on your product and your people.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. A written guide needs only your words.
