Three words that stopped me cold

I was reading hundreds of comments from people over 50 who want to start something new. One of them simply said: "Pivot to what?" This guide answers that question.

PART 1

Ask yourself 3 questions

What do people already ask you about? Do you want to show your face? Could you enjoy it for a whole year? The right idea usually jumps out after these.

PART 2

10 simple businesses

A "what I know" guide, printables, a kitchen-table newsletter, a faceless hobby page, a "life at 60" channel, honest recommendations, AI help for local businesses, teaching your old career skill, family memoirs, and a small paid community.

PART 3

What each one really takes

For every idea: who it fits, how AI helps, whether you need to show your face, how techy it is, and one small first step you can take this week.

PART 4

"Which one fits me?"

A simple chart that points you to your starting point, plus the copy-and-paste prompt that lets Claude ask you 5 questions and pick the best fit for you.
