Privacy Policy

The plain-language version of how this site handles your information.

What this site collects

Browsing this site requires no account, no sign-up, and no email address. The free guides and the free Quick-Start Guide are open — no email wall.

When you buy something

Purchases on this site are processed by Stripe, a secure payment company. Stripe handles your card details — I never see or store your card number. Stripe shares your name and email with me so I can deliver what you bought and answer your questions about your order. Stripe's own privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

Videos and outside services

Some pages embed videos from YouTube, which may set cookies when you play them. My community runs on Skool, and some of my tools run on their own sites — each has its own privacy policy that applies when you use it. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (like IP addresses) to run the service.

The masterclass heads-up list

On the blueprint page you can leave your email to hear when the free masterclass runs. It is optional and separate from the free guide, which never needs an email. I only add you if you type in your email and press the button, I only email you about the masterclass and my own free training, and every email has an unsubscribe link. Your email is kept in my email service, Brevo, and in a private record of when you gave permission. I do not sell or share it. To be removed, use the unsubscribe link or ask me and I will delete it.

What I don't do

I don't sell your information. I don't run third-party ad trackers on this site. I don't send you email unless you bought something (order delivery and support) or asked to hear from me.

Your choices

Want your information corrected or deleted? Ask me and I'll take care of it — message me inside the community or reply to your purchase receipt email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
